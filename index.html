About

Clinical medicine informed by evidence.

I trained in Emergency Medicine at Yale and am completing fellowship training in Critical Care Medicine at Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center. My academic background includes an MSc in Epidemiology and Biostatistics and research spanning emergency medicine, critical care, perioperative medicine, systematic review, meta-analysis, and outcomes research.
